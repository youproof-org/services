## ⚠ Decision needed — 506 KB-entity URLs + 213 taxonomy URLs

The theorem/definition/proof/remark pages (**506**) are individually indexed on the
legacy site but have **no standalone URL** on the new site — KB entities render
**inline within their embedding chapter** (`{chapter-url}#{id}`). The category/tag
pages (**213**) have no equivalent at all. Options (your call, drives redirect scope):

1. **301 each KB entity → its embedding chapter** (optionally `#anchor`). Preserves the
   most link equity but needs an entity→chapter map + stable anchors.
2. **301 KB entities → parent chapter (no anchor)**; let taxonomy pages `410`.
3. **Let all 719 `410`/404** — simplest, but drops ~700 indexed pages.

Only the **36 mapped items below** are in scope for Phase 5 slug work; the KB/taxonomy
redirect strategy is a separate decision (and likely a follow-up ticket).

## Recommendations for Phase 5

1. **Slugs** — the new narrative slugs (e.g. `alice-es-bob-szinrelep`) are cleaner and
   on-brand; the legacy ones were keyword-stuffed. Recommend **keeping the current new
   slugs** unless you want to fold specific keywords in. Either way the `legacy-path`
   301 covers the old URL, so decide purely on the merits of the new URL.
2. **Redirects** — no new redirect infra needed; the 36 `legacy-path`s already feed the
   manifest generator (verify in Phase 5).
3. **`meta` seeding (optional)** — consider copying the legacy `meta description` /
   `og:description` into the new `meta.description` / `meta.open-graph.description`
   for items where the new `excerpt` reads long or off-message. Do **not** copy og:image.
4. **KB/taxonomy (719 URLs)** — decide the redirect strategy above; likely a follow-up ticket.
